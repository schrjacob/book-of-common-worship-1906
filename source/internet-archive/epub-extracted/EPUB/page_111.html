THE ORDINATION OF DEACONS. 95 H Then the Minister shall say, NOW unto Him that is able to do exceedingly abundantly above all that we ask or think, according to the power that worketh in us; unto Him be glory in the Church by Christ Jesus, throughout all ages, world without end. Amen. If Where Deacons toho have already been ordained are to be installed in any congregation, The Order for their Installation may follow The Order for the Installation of Ruling Elders who have been previously Ordained.
