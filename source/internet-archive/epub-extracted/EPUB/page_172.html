OGOD, most merciful, who healest those that are broken in heart, and turnest the sadness of the sorrowful to joy ; Let Thy Fatherly goodness be upon all that Thou hast made. Especially we beseech Thee to remember in pity such as are this day destitute, homeless, or forgotten of their fellow-men. Bless the congregation of the poor. Uplift those who are cast down, mightily befriend innocent sufferers, and sanctify to them the endurance of their wrongs. Cheer with hope all discouraged and unhappy people, and by Thy heavenly grace preserve from falling those whose penury tempteth them to sin. Though they be troubled on every side, suffer them not to be distressed ; though they be perplexed, save them from despair. Grant this. O Lord, for the love of Him who for our sakes became poor, Thy Son, our Saviour Jesus Christ. Amen.
