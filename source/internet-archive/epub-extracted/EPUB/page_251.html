Psalm 123. 18 UNTO thee lift I up mine eyes: O thou that dwellest in the heavens. 19 Behold, as the eyes of servants look unto the hand of their masters, and as the eyes of a maiden unto the hand of her mistress: so our eyes wait upon the Lord our God, until that he have mercy upon us. 20 Have mercy upon us, O Lord, have mercy upon us: for we are exceedingly filled with contempt. 21 Our soul is exceedingly filled with the scorning of those that are at ease: and with the contempt of the proud.
