Therefore, this Church has from the beginning included in her government not only Ministers and Pastors, who are to preach the Gospel and administer the Sacraments in the Name of Christ and as His representatives: but also Ruling Elders chosen by the people to represent them, and to be joined with Pastors and Ministers in the exercise of government and discipline in the Church. These Ruling Elders in each congregation, together with the Pastor, constitute the Session, to whom it is committed to admit and exclude members, to regulate the worship, guard the doctrine, direct the activity, and conserve the interests of the congregation. The Elders are also to represent their brethren in Presbyteries, Synods, and General Assemblies, when commissioned thereto. And it is the duty of the Elders severally to set the example of a godly walk and conversation, and to assist the Pastor iu the visitation of the people, and in their spiritual guidance and comfort.
