SELECTION 47. Psalm 119: 97-120. 1 OHOW love I thy law: it is my meditation all the day. 2 Thou through thy commandments hast made me wiser than mine enemies : for they are ever with me. 3 I have more understanding than all my teachers : for thy testimonies are my meditation. 4 I understand more than the ancients : because I keep thy precepts. 5 I have refrained my feet from every evil way : that I might keep thy word. 6 I have not departed from thy judgments: for thou hast taught me. 7 How sweet are thy words unto my taste : yea, sweeter than honey to my mouth. 8 Through thy precepts I get understanding : therefore I hate every false way 9 Thy word is a lamp unto my feet : and a light unto my path. 10 I have sworn, and I will perform it: that I will keep thy righteous judgments. 11 I am afflicted very much: quicken me, 0 Lord, according unto thy word. 12 Accept, I beseech thee, the free-will offerings of my mouth, O Lord: and teach me thy judgments. 13 My soul is continually in my hand: yet do I not forget thy law. 14 The wicked have laid a snare for me: yet I erred not from thy precepts. 15 Thy testimonies have I taken as a heritage for ever: for they are the rejoicing of my heart. 16 I have inclined mine heart to perform thy statutes always : even unto the end. 17 I hate vain thoughts : but thy law do I love.
