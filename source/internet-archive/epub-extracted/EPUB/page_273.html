We praise Thee, we bless Thee, we worship Thee: we glorify Thee, we give thanks to Thee for Thy great glory, O Lord God, heavenly King: God the Father Almighty. O Lord, the only-begotten Son, Jesus Christ: O Lord God, Lamb of God, Son of the Father, That takest away the sins of the world: have mercy upon us. Thou that takest away the sins of the world : receive our prayer. Thou that sittest at the right hand of God the Father: have mercy upon us. For Thou only art holy : Thou only art the Lord. Thou only, O Christ, with the Holy Ghost: art most high in the glory of God the Father. Amen.
