Psalm 26: 8-12. 1 LORD, I have loved the habitation of thy house : and the place where thine honour dwelleth. 2 Gather not my soul with sinners: nor my life with bloody men, 3 In whose hands is mischief: and their right hand is full of bribes. 4 But as for me, I will walk in mine integrity : redeem me, and be merciful unto me. 5 My foot standeth in an even place : in the congregations will I bless the Lord.
