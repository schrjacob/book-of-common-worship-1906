SELECTION 60. 253 26 For the Lord heareth the poor: and despiseth not his prisoners, 27 Let the heaven and earth praise him: the seas, and every thing that moveth therein. 28 For God will save Zion, and will build the cities of Jiidah : that they may dwell there, and have it in possession. 29 The seed also of his servants shall inherit it : and they that love his name shall dwell therein.
