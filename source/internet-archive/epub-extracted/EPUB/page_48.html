Lord, be gracious unto us, and help us to obtain this blessing. Blessed are they which do hunger and thirst after righteousness : for they shall be filled. Lord, be gracious unto us, and help us to obtain this blessing. Blessed are the merciful : for they shall obtain mercy. Lord, be gracious unto us, and help us to obtain this blessing. Blessed are the pure in heart : for they shall see God. Lord, be gracious unto us, and help us to obtain this blessing. Blessed are the peacemakers : for they shall be called the children of God. Lord, be gracious unto us, and help us to obtain this blessing. Blessed are they which are persecuted for righteousness' sake : for theirs is the kingdom of heaven.
